About Roof Ninjas LTD

BUILT ON TRUST.
DRIVEN BY CRAFT.

Founded in 2013 by Will and Beth, Roof Ninjas LTD has grown from a small local crew into one of Halifax's most trusted roofing companies. Every project gets the same treatment: thorough inspection, honest pricing, and craftsmanship that speaks for itself. We proudly use hand nail application on every job — no shortcuts, just precision.

What We Do

ROOFING SERVICES

From shingle replacements to full commercial builds, we handle every aspect of your roofing project.

Residential Roofing

Complete roof replacements, repairs, and new installations for homes of all sizes. We work with all shingle types and styles.

Commercial Roofing

Large-scale commercial projects from retail plazas to institutional buildings. Experienced with flat roofs, metal, and specialty systems.

Insurance Claims

We work directly with your insurance company to make storm damage claims painless. Full documentation and assessment provided.

Roof Inspections

Thorough inspections with detailed reporting. We identify problems early so you can plan repairs before they become emergencies.

Roof Maintenance

Preventative maintenance programs to extend the life of your roof. Regular check-ups catch small issues before they become costly.

New Construction

Partnering with builders on new home and commercial construction projects. Quality installations that protect your investment from day one.
